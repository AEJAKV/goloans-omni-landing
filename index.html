So what’s the catch?

The only condition is timing. Your loan has a 91 to 120 day term, and if you pay off the full $500 within 14 days, a single $1 debit fee is your whole cost of borrowing. The one optional extra is express deposit: if you want your money the same day, an additional charge of $25–$35 applies. Every cost is listed in your agreement before you sign, so nothing shows up later.

I already applied. Why do I need to verify my bank?

What if I can’t pay it off within 14 days?

That’s fine. You keep repaying on your regular schedule over the loan’s 91 to 120 day term, and the standard cost of borrowing applies instead of the $1 offer. See the representative example. Questions? Call us at 1-866-478-4119.

How long is the loan?

Every loan has a term of 91 to 120 days, depending on how often you’re paid. You can pay it off early at any time, and paying the full $500 within 14 days is what gets you the $1 total cost.

Is express deposit required?

No. Express deposit is an optional paid upgrade for same-day funding, with an additional charge of $25–$35. Skip it and your fee stays at $1.

Who can apply?

You need to be 18 or older, with a steady source of income and an active Canadian bank account.

Will applying hurt my credit score?

We don’t run a hard credit check to review your application.

How fast will I hear back?

Most applicants get an answer the same day when they apply during business hours.
